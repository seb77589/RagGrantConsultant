## Why vector results are not required to match exactly
HNSW is an approximate index. A restore rebuilds the graph with a different
insertion order, so nearest-neighbour results differ at the cut-off even
though the data is byte-identical -- which the checksum above demonstrates.
Every section returned on one side but not the other was verified to exist
on both sides and to score within 0.02 of the last result that made the top
ten: ranking noise at the boundary, not lost data. Demanding exact equality
here would test the index's determinism rather than the migration.
