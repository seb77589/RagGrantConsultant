About the data

What this service holds, where it came from, and what it is not.

Sources and attribution

This service retrieves and quotes material published by the European Union and its bodies. © European Union. Source material is reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU on the reuse of Commission documents, and under the licence stated by each publisher. Every answer cites the document it drew on, with a link to the official source and the date that source was published.

Current sources:

  • CORDIS — project records for Horizon Europe, CC BY 4.0.

How the text has been handled

The text shown here is not a verbatim reproduction of a whole official document. Source documents have been extracted, split into sections and summarised so that relevant passages can be retrieved and quoted. Structured fields such as funding amounts, dates, country and region codes are also rendered into English sentences for retrieval. Always consult the linked official source before relying on anything stated here.

Each cited section carries the date its source was last changed upstream and the date this service fetched it. Where a source is old, the answer says so.

Personal data

Person-level information is removed at ingestion. National Contact Point names, project participant contacts, principal investigators and similar personal fields are discarded rather than stored. Organisation names and official organisation URLs are kept, because they are part of the public record of who received funding.

Independence

This is an independent service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the European Commission or any European Union institution, and it does not use the EU emblem or any Commission logo. It provides no legal advice and no guarantee of eligibility or funding. Decisions about eligibility, deadlines and funding rates must be verified against the official call documentation and the responsible national or European authority.

Figures, funding rates and deadlines are only ever reported when they appear in the retrieved source text, and are shown with the supporting quotation. If the retrieved material does not contain an answer, the service says so rather than estimating one.
